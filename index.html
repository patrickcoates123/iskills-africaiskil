A region of opportunity.
A market-by-market approach.

Successful market entry starts with understanding where your solution can make a meaningful difference.

Sub-Saharan Africa is a diverse region. Education priorities, institutional structures, procurement processes and delivery conditions vary between countries — and between organisations within them.

We help you identify the best fit between what you offer and what customers need. From early market assessment to active business development, our role is to turn regional ambition into a focused, practical plan.

About ISkills Africa

Relationships matter.
Understanding makes
them useful.

Patrick CoatesInternational Skills UK
Erica HepburnInternational Skills UK

ISkills Africa is the proposed Africa-facing brand of International Skills UK Ltd, an education technology consultancy focused on sales, market entry and partnership development in Anglophone sub-Saharan Africa.

Our work draws on Patrick Coates and Erica Hepburn’s experience of working across the region, established relationships and an understanding of the differences between local markets.

We take a strategic approach: understand the customer’s needs, assess where a supplier can add value, and develop a route to market that fits both. That can mean advising your team directly, acting as a representative or agent, or sourcing partners to support local sales and delivery.

We bring a practical perspective to both sides of the relationship — helping international providers understand local priorities and helping potential customers understand the value of a relevant solution.

Useful to know

Questions before we start.

Do we need to have chosen a country already?+

No. We can help you assess countries, customer groups and routes to market before committing resources.

Can you work alongside our own sales team?+

Yes. We can extend your team with local market insight, stakeholder access and practical follow-up.

Can you find a local agent or implementation partner?+

Yes, subject to the markets and requirements of the engagement. We assess partner fit and can support early discussions.

Do you only work with software companies?+

No. We work with education and assessment technology, services and solution providers seeking a regional route to market.

Do introductions guarantee a contract?+

No. Our role is to build relevant conversations and support opportunity development; procurement decisions remain with the customer.

How do you manage confidentiality and conflicts?+

We agree confidentiality, scope and conflict-management arrangements before work begins.
